A wanderer between worlds — of reason and wonder, business and art, science and spirit. I help ideas grow through strategy and structure, yet never stop chasing the magic in the ordinary. The Chronicles of The Wanderer

“To walk is to become - every step a lesson, every moment a memory shaping the soul”

1989

Born

Beneath a restless sky, a quiet spark ignited — the first breath of a story destined to wander far and dream deep.

July 1995

First steps into learning

A child with bright eyes, stepping into a world of chalk dust, laughter, and small beginnings.

October 2000

The girl on the stairs

A moment etched in eternity — I saw her for the first time. My heart knew before I did; she would become my destiny.

2001 — 2004

Growing through seasons

iddle school, high school — years of discovery and silent dreams chasing the horizon.

2005 — 2006

First taste of work

From surfwear counters to consulting tables — I learned the rhythm of effort, the beauty of earning one’s way.

July 2007

A reunion written by fate

University opened more than doors — it brought her back to me. This time, I spoke, and our story began anew.

2008 — 2009

The courage to love

Between pages and deadlines, I found the courage to ask her to be mine. April 2009 — love answered softly, yes.

2010

Across oceans

She left for Australia to chase her dreams. Months later, I followed — not to chase, but to walk beside her once more.

2011 — 2013

Learning, building, becoming

Degrees earned, lessons learned, and late nights woven with hope. Together, we built the foundation of who we are.

2014

Vows under open skies

April brought a promise; May, a wedding. Two souls bound by love, faith, and shared tomorrows.

2015 — 2016

The birth of family

Our first daughter was born in Australia. We returned home, carrying dreams and lullabies in our hearts.

2016 — 2018

Winds of change

From corporate desks to new ventures, I learned the art of rebuilding — that endings often hide beginnings.

2019 — 2020

Roots and blossoms

A first home, a second daughter. The laughter of children filling the air — proof that love multiplies quietly.

2021 — 2023

Purpose and Prosperity

I stepped into a realm of leadership, guiding with vision and calm conviction. I became an investor in dreams — nurturing ventures that echoed growth and grace. Another home followed, a quiet testament to patience, vision, and arrival.

July 2025

A third light

Our third daughter arrived — a new melody in the song of our family, completing the circle of grace and love.

The Reveries of The Wanderer

“To pause is to feel - to let stillness speak in colors, sounds, and quite wonder”

Watching Movies or TV series

Cinema is the art of remembering what we never lived. In every flicker of light, I see myself — a witness to love, loss, and the fragile theater of being human.

Driving

The road is my meditation. In the hum of the engine and the blur of passing miles, I find clarity — the kind that only comes when the horizon keeps running from you.

Cooking

In fire and fragrance, I tell stories without words. Each dish is a memory, each flavor a tender echo of warmth, care, and the simple holiness of sharing.

Gaming

Worlds unfold before me — vast, unreal, yet achingly true. Through pixels and quests, I chase courage, wonder, and the quiet joy of victory earned in silence.

Songwriting / Music Composing

When emotions hum too loud for speech, I turn them into melodies. Each lyric is a memory reborn in sound, each note, a pulse of something that once lived quietly inside me.

Reading

Books are constellations bound in paper — and I, a wanderer among their stars. Through them, I travel centuries without moving, and return wiser, softer, infinitely more human.

Tinkering & DIY Projects

Creation, to me, is sacred labor — the merging of patience and curiosity. I tinker not to fix, but to understand; not to build, but to listen to how the world fits together in small, stubborn miracles.

Hiking & Camping

Among the trees and the sky’s great silences, I find my truest rhythm. The earth hums beneath my steps, rivers echo in my veins — and I remember: I, too, am a part of this breathing wilderness.

Fishing

The lake teaches me what time cannot — that stillness is not the absence of motion, but the presence of peace. Each cast a prayer, each ripple a fleeting answer.

Coding / Programming

I speak to machines in languages of logic and light. To code is to dream in structure — to summon order from chaos, to sculpt the invisible with intent.

Painting / Digital Art

In the hush between thought and breath, I paint what words cannot hold. Each stroke - a heartbeat of color, each shape - a whisper of dream. On canvas or screen, I chase light until it remembers my presence.

Writing Poem & Essay

I write to unburden the soul - to give sorrow a rhythm and silence a tongue. Poetry becomes my confession, essays, my conversation with eternity. In ink, I rediscover the architecture of feeling

Frederick Dominico - 2020
